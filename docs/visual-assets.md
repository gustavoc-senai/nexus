# Nexus visual assets

The new orbital observatory image was generated with the built-in imagegen tool, then encoded as JPEG at quality 88 for website delivery.

Files: docs/assets/nexus-observatory-source.png (source), public/images/nexus-observatory.jpg (web asset).

Prompt: Refined photorealistic architectural visualization of a sophisticated orbital research observatory, brushed metal and graphite, glass windows overlooking Earth, restrained teal lighting, geometric physical prism, pyramid and sphere models on the right, calm dark left third for HTML headings. Mature scientific institution, realistic materials, soft daylight, no people, text, logos, watermarks or cartoons. Landscape 1536 × 1024.

Eight geometry-*.svg covers were built from the same mathematical meshes as the 3D laboratory, with local vector output. Decorative image alternatives are empty because the adjacent titles convey their meaning.
